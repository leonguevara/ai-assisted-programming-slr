You are assisting in the refinement of a multidimensional Systematic Literature Review (SLR) on AI-assisted programming in software engineering.

The attached Excel workbook contains the final curated evidence base after:

* PRISMA screening,
* manual recovery of discarded studies,
* targeted Scopus supplementation,
* dimensional balancing,
* and duplicate/full-text filtering.

IMPORTANT:
All papers currently in the “Corpus” sheet are already considered valid primary studies.
This is NOT a PRISMA exclusion task.
Do NOT re-evaluate inclusion/exclusion eligibility from scratch.

Your task is to perform a strategic epistemic segmentation of the corpus.

The goal is to distinguish between:

* CORE papers (structurally essential to the synthesis),
* SUPPORTING papers (useful complementary evidence),
* PERIPHERAL papers (limited marginal contribution),
* and REMOVE_CANDIDATE papers (high redundancy or low analytical contribution).

You must think dimensionally and structurally, not paper-by-paper.

The SLR uses the following dimensions:

* Productivity
* Code Quality & Technical Debt
* Human–AI Interaction
* Perception & Trust
* Economic Impact
* Organizational Impact
* Cognitive Load / Human Factors

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS:

* Do NOT disproportionately weaken dimensions with already limited support (especially Economic Impact, Organizational Impact, Cognitive Load/Human Factors, and Perception & Trust).
* Papers providing unique conceptual or empirical support to weaker dimensions should be preserved, even if their methodology is less rigorous.
* Do NOT use venue prestige as a primary criterion.
* Avoid over-valuing benchmark-heavy papers that provide redundant findings.
* Favor papers with:

  * ecological validity,
  * real-world developer behavior,
  * longitudinal evidence,
  * industrial contexts,
  * multidimensional findings,
  * socio-technical richness,
  * strong methodological grounding,
  * or unique conceptual contributions.

For each paper in the Corpus sheet, provide:

1. Segmentation category:

   * CORE
   * SUPPORTING
   * PERIPHERAL
   * REMOVE_CANDIDATE

2. Brief rationale (1–3 sentences)

3. Main strengths

4. Main weaknesses

5. Whether removing the paper would weaken any underrepresented dimension

6. Redundancy level:

   * LOW
   * MEDIUM
   * HIGH

7. Papers or themes it substantially overlaps with

You should prioritize:

* synthesis quality,
* conceptual coherence,
* dimensional balance,
* and analytical richness,
  over raw corpus size.

The objective is NOT to maximize reduction.
The objective is to maximize explanatory power and synthesis coherence.
